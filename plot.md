## Plot

### Exposition

While guarding the princess the character is daydreaming about the old days, when they
were a newbie. Older knight is asking character who they are and who the player is,
weirdly enough. Kingdom of Silver is slightly described by the older knight. Older knight
teaches character how to fight.

### Inciting Incident

As a window shatters, the character is snapped out of their daydream. As the character and
the old knight enter the room, they see a vampire next to the princess. The character and
the old knight lunge at the vampire and are defeated instantly. The vampire takes a bow
and takes the princess with him. After the shocking event, the character reorients
themself. They look at the old knight and see that he is no longer alive. While in shock
Another knight comes in to see what happened. After looking at the scene, the knight sounds
the alarm and gets everyone to secure the castle.

### Rising Action

After all of that happens, the king summons the character. He gives the knights an order "find
the princess and save her, no matter the cost". He then personally calls up the character
and tells them to deliver a letter to the giant king, asking for help.

The character then goes on a journey to the Giant King's Court. On the way they pass by
their hometown(Afternoon Town), which has been plagued by a giant tree. The character
can choose to cut it down or not. After dealing with the tree or not, the character moves
on towards the giant king's court.

The character goes towards the giant king's court gate and is immediately stopped by two
giants. The giants ask why the character is standing in a sacred area. After explaining,
the character is ushered inside by one of the giants. The giant tells the character to
not look at the king's face or else they risk facing eternal darkness. The character
reaches the inner court and is asked why they're in the court. The character says who
they are and why they're in the court. The character gives the giant king the letter.
The giant king reads the letter then laughs a bit. He then says he will not help them at
all. After some silence, the giant queen clears her throat and convinces the giant king
to somewhat help the kingdom of silver. The king calls out another giant and tells the
giant to bring the potions. The character is then told to choose one and only one. After
picking one the king orders the character to drink the potion. After the character drinks
the potion they collapse and have weird visions. One of them explaining how their new
abilities work and the other being an indecipherable message.

After a day, the character wakes up on a large bed. The first thing they see is a giant
sitting next to them, happy to see that they're alive. The giant then goes on to explain
what happened to them. The character now has enough strength to go and save the princess.
That is all the help that the character will get from the giant king. They are also told
that the princess may be in Moon city or Nois City.

It doesn't matter which city the character goes to. In either city they will encounter
a giant tree, but this time the giant fruits have split open. There are devils
terrorizing the city.
