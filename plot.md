## Characters

**You (mc)**: Rookie knight of the kingdom of silver, looking to prove yourself.

**Princess**: Princess of the kingdom of silver.

**Zedus**: A vampire. Follower of the Mother Goddess of Depravity

**Mother Goddess of Depravity**: BBEG. Wants to invade earth. Speaks in assembly. Also
known as: Primordial Moon, The Great Mother, Sovereign of the Physical World, and the
Mother of All Things.

**King**: king of the kingdom of silver. He's old and only cares about his daughter.

**Aurmir**: King of kings. Resides in the Giant King's Court.

**Omebella**: Giant queen. Wife of Aurmir.
